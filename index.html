Website Guidelines

Welcome to the PropMan Lobby!

This guide covers everything — from tracking residency hours to requesting folios. Use the quick-jump below or scroll through.

Getting Started

  • Sign in with your official @dlsu.edu.ph Google account.
  • Toggle between Light and Dark mode using the sun/moon icon in the header.
  • The Online and Onsite mini-boxes show how many people are currently active.
  • Open the Account Information menu to edit your profile — Position, Section, Full Name, Nickname, Pronouns, and ID Number.
  • Use the Send Feedback / Report Bug button (bug icon) to submit ratings, feedback, or bug reports.
  • There are a few hidden surprises ("easter eggs") tucked into the site — keep an eye on things like the theme button and the brand logo. See if you can find them all!

Residency Requirements

There is a minimum number of residency hours you must complete each week and each term. These can be accomplished Online or Onsite (F2F) — or a mix of both.

Residency Role Per Week Per Term
Newbies 5 hours 30 hours
Regularized 2.5 hours 15 hours
Beyond these, you can earn extra residency hours by attending workshops and events, alongside submitting drafts.

Residency Tracker

Clocking In
  • Click the green Clock In button, then choose Onsite or Online.
  • Select at least one task from the "Accomplishing Tasks" options (Drafts, Academics, Event Preparation, Hanging Out, Misc. Org. Activity) — or toggle Other: to type a custom task. Choosing at least one is required to clock in, and multiple tasks can be selected. Your selections are saved with your clock-in log.
  • A live duration counter will appear showing how long you've been clocked in.
Onsite clock-in is closed outside 7 AM — 10 PM on Mondays to Saturdays and all day on Sundays (campus/office closed). During those times you can still clock in Online anytime. If you're an EB and absolutely need to, you can override an onsite clock-in with a confirmation prompt.
Clocking Out
  • When you are done, click the red Clock Out button.
  • Your session ends, total hours update, and the timer disappears.
Important: Always remember to clock out when you're done! The system auto-clocks out Onsite shifts at 10 PM as a safety net. Online sessions keep running until you clock out yourself, so don't forget to click Clock Out.
Tracking Your Activity
  • Your main panel shows your last 5 logs — clock-ins and outs with mode (Online/Onsite) and any notes you added.
  • Select Log History from the menu to see your complete record with dates and timestamps.

Get a Folio

Browsing
  • Each folio card shows an image, the folio name, the remaining stock (labeled Total), and the per-member limit.
  • Use the search bar to filter folios by name.
  • Click on a folio image to open a full-size preview.
Ordering
  • Use the + and − buttons to set the quantity for each folio. The input caps at the folio's distribution limit, so you can't order more than the maximum quantity set by the EBs.
  • The round cart button at the bottom-right opens a slide-out panel with a summary of all selected items and quantities.
  • Click Get Selected to submit your request.
Quantity Limits & EB Password
  • Each folio type has a per-member limit set by the EBs. If a folio has a limit of 3, you can only take up to 3 of that folio type in total.
  • If you want more than the per-member limit, you'll need an EB password to proceed.
  • Once verified, the approval lasts for your entire session — you won't need to re-enter it.
Folios marked DO NOT DISTRIBUTE are not available for distribution. Contact an EB if you think this is a mistake.

EB Password System

  • EBs can set a personal password from the Admin Board. It can be anything, make it fun!
  • This password lets members approve folio requests over the per-member limit. If you're a member, ask an EB for their password.
  • Passwords reset every Saturday at 10 PM for security. EBs will need to set a new one after reset.
  • If an EB hasn't set a password yet, they'll be prompted to create one when they log in.

Feedback & Bug Reports

  • Click the bug icon in the header to open the feedback modal.
  • Switch between Feedback (with star ratings) or Bug Report mode.
  • For feedback, rate your experience and how you found the website — both star ratings are required.
  • For bug reports, describe the bug you encountered and include steps to reproduce if possible.
  • Write a message and hit Submit — we read every submission!

Guide for EBs

The Admin Board option in the menu gives you extra tools to manage the roster and members.

Active Sessions

The Roster panel lists every member with their current status (In = clocked in, Out = clocked out). Use the Clock Out All button at the top of the Roster to force clock out every active member at once.

Roster Management
  • Shows all members, their total verified hours, a status chip (In = clocked in, Out = clocked out), and their section.
  • Open the Filter drawer to search by name or email, filter by section, or sort the list Clocked In First / Clocked Out First.
  • Click on a member to view their full account information.
  • Force In / Force Out: Manually toggle a member's status if they forgot to clock in or out.
  • Purge: Completely wipe a user's log history and hours. This is permanent — use with care!
Log Missed Hours

If a member forgot to clock in, use the Log Missed Hours section to add hours directly. Search for the member by name or email, choose + Add or − Deduct, then enter the hours and minutes. This logs an entry in the Res_Log sheet.

Assigning New Admins

Type a @dlsu.edu.ph email in the "Give Admin Privileges" field and click Add. They'll gain administrative access instantly.

Set Your EB Password
  • In the Admin Board, you'll find a section to set your EB Password.
  • This password is used when members need to request more than the per-folio limit. Share it with them when they ask!
  • Passwords reset every Saturday at 10 PM, so you'll need to set a new one each week.
  • If you haven't set one yet, you'll see a prompt when you log in.
Manage the Folio Shop
  • Click the pencil icon on any folio card to open the Edit Folio modal.
  • You can update the Beginning Qty, Limit per Member, Location, Note, and Image URL.
  • Use Save Changes to update the folio inventory or correct a folio's details.
Folio Bypass
  • Hover over a DO NOT DISTRIBUTE folio — it'll show "BYPASS?". Click it to add it to your cart.

Reminders

  • Always clock out when you're done! Onsite shifts auto-clock-out at 10 PM as a safety net, but Online sessions keep running until you clock out manually — so manual clock-out keeps your hours most accurate.
  • When only a few people are left in the office, an Office Closure Reminder may appear after someone clocks out — it asks you to turn off the lights, unplug extension cords, and switch off the air conditioner before you go.
  • If your total hours or occupancy numbers don't update immediately, refresh the page to pull the latest data.
  • Think something is wrong? Contact an EB or submit a Bug Report from the header.
